About me

I am a junior undergraduate student at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Zhejiang University of Technology (ZJUT). I am dedicated to the frontier exploration of Physical AI, building general intelligence systems with deep physical semantic understanding and robust interaction capabilities in complex environments. I welcome discussions and potential collaborations—please feel free to reach out. I am advised by Prof. Jianwei Zheng (College of Computer Science and Technology, ZJUT).
